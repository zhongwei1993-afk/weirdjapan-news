## What's Actually Going On

Lost-and-found in Japan is a national obsession. Tokyo Metro returns 80% of lost umbrellas. The system is run by retirees who genuinely care. What seems random is usually the product of a very specific combination of circumstances: limited urban space, an aging but disciplined population, a deep cultural tolerance for ritualized service, and a national appetite for slightly excessive specialization.

In most countries, this kind of thing would be a tourist curiosity. In Japan, it's infrastructure. It's normal. People use it on Tuesday mornings without thinking about it.

And that — the unselfconscious normalcy — is what makes it so disorienting to visitors.
